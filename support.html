How to play Woodline

  1. Place a piece.
    Drag a block from below the board into an empty space.
  2. Complete a line.
    Fill a horizontal or vertical line to clear its blocks and earn points.
  3. Make a little room.
    Leave space for your next pieces as you work toward a new personal best.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to play?

No. Woodline is designed to let you start playing without signing in. In the current version, Google and Apple account linking is a demonstration only and does not connect a real account.

Will my progress be saved?

Your board and score are saved on your current device. When you return and select Play, you can choose to continue. Uninstalling the app or clearing its stored data may remove your progress.

How do I start a new game?

Tap the restart button during a game, then select the option to start a new game. This replaces your current progress. To keep playing your current board, select the option to continue.

Can I change the sound and theme?

Use the speaker icon in the game to turn sound effects on or off. Tap the sun icon on the main screen to switch between light and dark mode.

Can I move my progress to another device?

The current version saves progress locally. Real account linking and synchronization between devices are not available.
