LASTROAD / PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Data stored on your device

LastRoad does not require an account or sign-in. Your story progress, character status and reading settings are stored on your device only. Sailaway operates no server for the game and does not receive this data.

Advertising

The app shows advertisements. They are served through AdMob, a service of Google LLC, which may fill them through partner ad networks that take part in mediation, including Unity Ads from Unity Technologies and OpenX.

With each ad request, the Google Mobile Ads SDK and the SDKs of participating ad networks may receive the device advertising identifier, your IP address and the approximate location inferred from it, information about ads viewed and tapped, and device and diagnostic information. Sailaway does not combine this data with any other data it holds.

Each advertising partner handles this data under its own privacy policy: Google Privacy Policy and Unity Privacy Policy. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a consent form is shown before ads are loaded, and your choice is respected.

Retention and deletion

Deleting the app erases everything the game has stored on your device. You can reset the advertising identifier, or limit ad personalization, in your device settings.

Children

LastRoad contains violence and survival-horror themes and is not directed at children.
