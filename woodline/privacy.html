Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Account-linking information

In the current version, Google and Apple account linking is a demonstration only. It does not connect a real account, and we do not collect or store account IDs, names, email addresses, passwords, or authentication tokens through this feature.

Only the account provider selected during the demonstration (Google or Apple) is saved in browser storage on your current device. This is used solely to display the demonstration status on the account-linking screen and is not sent to an external server.

You can remove this information using the option to clear account-linking demonstration data on the account-linking screen, or by clearing the relevant browser storage. This does not delete or unlink a real Google or Apple account.
