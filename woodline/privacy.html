Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Account sign-in information

When you sign in with a Google or Apple account, the app receives the account identifier and display name that the service provides, and the email address where the account provides one. Sailaway operates no server; what is received is stored on your device only and is not transmitted anywhere.

Passwords are never received under any circumstances. If you chose to hide your email with an Apple account, only the relay address Apple provides is shown.

Game progress and your personal best are stored on your device regardless of any account. Connecting an account does not sync between devices or back up to a server.

Advertising

This app shows a banner advertisement at the top of the screen and an occasional full-screen advertisement at the end of a round. Advertisements are served through AdMob, a service of Google LLC, which may fill them through partner ad networks that take part in AdMob mediation, including Unity Ads from Unity Technologies. The app requests non-personalized ads only. Non-personalized ads are not based on your past behaviour, but advertising services still use the device advertising identifier for frequency capping, aggregated reporting and fraud prevention.

With each ad request, the Google Mobile Ads SDK, and the SDKs of participating ad networks such as Unity Ads, may receive the device advertising identifier, your IP address and the approximate location inferred from it, information about ads viewed and tapped, and device and diagnostic information. Sailaway does not combine the advertising identifier with any other data it holds.

Each advertising partner handles this data under its own privacy policy: Google Privacy Policy and Unity Privacy Policy. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form is shown before any ad is loaded.

Retention and deletion

Disconnect on the account screen erases the stored account details and keeps your records. Delete account erases the account details together with the personal best and saved game on your device. Deleting the app also erases everything stored on the device.

You can reset the advertising identifier, or limit ad personalization, in your device settings.
